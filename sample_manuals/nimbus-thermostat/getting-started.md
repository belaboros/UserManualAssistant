# Nimbus Thermostat

The Nimbus Thermostat is a wall-mounted smart thermostat for central heating and cooling systems. This manual explains how to install the thermostat, set it up and keep it running. Connectivity, firmware and troubleshooting are described in the settings guide.

## What is in the box

- Nimbus Thermostat display unit
- Wall plate with built-in bubble level
- Two mounting screws and two wall anchors
- Trim plate for covering old wall marks
- Wire labels (R, W, Y, G, C)
- Quick start card

## Before you install

Switch off the power to your heating and cooling system at the circuit breaker before you touch any wires. The thermostat works with 24 V low-voltage systems. It does not work with 120 V or 240 V line-voltage baseboard heaters.

Take a photo of your existing wiring and use the wire labels to mark each wire as you disconnect it. The label must match the letter printed on the terminal the wire was connected to, not the color of the wire.

## Installation

1. Remove the cover of your old thermostat and unscrew its base from the wall.
2. Attach the Nimbus wall plate with the two screws. Use the built-in bubble level so that the plate is straight.
3. Push each labeled wire into the matching terminal on the wall plate. The terminal latches when the wire is fully inserted. Gently pull each wire to check it is secure.
4. If your system has a C wire (common wire), connect it to the C terminal. The thermostat needs a C wire for stable operation of its display and Wi-Fi radio.
5. Align the display unit with the wall plate and press it on until it clicks.
6. Restore power at the circuit breaker. The display lights up within 30 seconds.

## First-time setup

When the thermostat starts for the first time, a setup assistant guides you through these choices:

1. **Language.** Turn the dial to select a language and press the dial to confirm.
2. **System type.** Choose heating only, cooling only, or heating and cooling.
3. **Temperature unit.** Choose Celsius or Fahrenheit.
4. **Wi-Fi.** Select your home network and enter the password. You can skip this step and complete it later. See the Wi-Fi section of the settings guide.

After setup the display shows the current room temperature and the target temperature.

## Using the dial

Turn the dial to change the target temperature. Press the dial once to open the menu. Press and hold the dial for two seconds to switch the thermostat between Home and Away modes. The ring around the dial glows orange while the system heats, blue while it cools, and stays dark when it is idle.

## Schedules

A schedule tells the thermostat which temperature to hold at which time. The default schedule has four periods on weekdays and two on weekends.

To create or change a schedule on the thermostat:

1. Press the dial and choose **Menu > Schedule**.
2. Select the day or group of days you want to edit.
3. Select a period and set its start time and target temperature.
4. Press the dial to save.

You can add up to six periods per day. If two periods overlap, the thermostat uses the one that starts later. Schedules continue to run when the thermostat has no network connection.

### Temporary hold

Turning the dial during a scheduled period sets a temporary hold. The hold lasts until the start of the next period. To cancel the hold early, press the dial and choose **Resume schedule**.

## Away mode and eco limits

In Away mode the thermostat keeps the house between a minimum and a maximum temperature to save energy. The defaults are 16 C (61 F) minimum and 27 C (81 F) maximum. You can change these limits under **Menu > Eco limits**. The minimum can never be set below 7 C (45 F), which protects pipes from freezing.
