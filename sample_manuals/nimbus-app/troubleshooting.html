Troubleshooting in the Nimbus app

This page helps you solve common problems with the Nimbus app and with devices that are connected to it.

The app cannot find my device

Make sure your phone's Bluetooth is on and that the app is allowed to use it. Stay within 3 meters of the device. Close the app completely and open it again. If the device is a thermostat, check that its display is on and that it is not showing an error code.

A device shows as offline

An offline device has lost its connection to your Wi-Fi network or to the hub. Check that your router is on. If you use a hub, check that the hub LED is solid green. An amber blinking LED means the hub has no internet connection. Devices that are paired with a hub keep running their schedules while offline, but the app cannot change them until the connection is back.

Notifications do not arrive

Open your phone settings and check that notifications are allowed for the Nimbus app. In the app, open Settings > Notifications and make sure the alert types you want are turned on. Battery saver modes on some phones delay notifications. Add the app to the list of apps that are not restricted.

The code does not match

When you add a thermostat, the 6-digit code in the app must match the code on the thermostat display. If the codes differ, you are probably connecting to a different thermostat or hub that is also in pairing mode nearby. Cancel the pairing, make sure only one device is in pairing mode, and start again.

Reset the thermostat

If the thermostat is unresponsive or does not pair, you can reset it to factory settings. A reset erases the thermostat's schedules, Wi-Fi password and hub pairing.

  1. Remove the display unit from the wall plate.
  2. Use a paper clip to press and hold the reset pin on the back for 5 seconds, until the display shows the Nimbus logo.
  3. Release the pin and attach the display unit to the wall plate again.
  4. In the app, remove the old thermostat entry under Devices and add the thermostat again.

Reset the hub from the app

Open the hub in the app and choose Hub settings > Network > Reset network. This removes the hub's Wi-Fi settings but keeps paired devices.

Sign-in problems

If you forgot your password, choose Forgot password on the sign-in screen. We send a link to your email address. The link works for 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and make sure you used the address you registered with.

Contact support

If these steps do not solve your problem, open Settings > Help > Contact support in the app. Include the model of the device and any error code that it shows. The app attaches the firmware versions of your devices to your message automatically.
