Pairing devices in the Nimbus app

The Nimbus app is the main way to control your Nimbus devices from your phone. This page explains how to create an account, add a hub and add devices such as the Nimbus Thermostat.

Install the app and sign in

Download the Nimbus app from your phone's app store. Open the app and choose Create account. Enter your email address and a password of at least 10 characters. We send a confirmation email. Open the link in that email, then return to the app and sign in.

The app works on phones with a recent version of iOS or Android. You need to allow notifications to get alerts when a device reports a problem.

Add a hub

A hub is optional for devices that use Wi-Fi, but it is needed for the mesh connection and for remote control when your Wi-Fi is unreliable.

  1. Power on the hub and wait until its LED is solid green.
  2. In the app, open Devices, choose Add and then Hub.
  3. Allow the app to use Bluetooth when asked. The app finds the hub nearby.
  4. Give the hub a name, for example "Living room hub", and choose Save.

Add a thermostat

You can add a thermostat to the app directly over Wi-Fi, or through a hub. To add it through a hub, complete the steps on the hub and on the thermostat first, as described in their manuals. Then finish in the app:

  1. Open the app and choose Devices, then Add, then Thermostat.
  2. The app shows a 6-digit code. Compare it with the code on the thermostat display.
  3. If both codes match, choose Confirm. If they differ, choose Cancel and start again.
  4. Give the thermostat a name and choose the room it is in.
  5. The thermostat appears in your device list with its current temperature.

The code expires after two minutes. If it expires, start again from Devices > Add > Thermostat.

Rooms and names

Group devices into rooms to control them together. Open Devices, choose a device, then Room. You can create new rooms from the same screen. A room can hold devices of different types, and each device can be in only one room.

Share access with your household

You can invite other people to control your devices. Open Settings > Household > Invite and enter their email address. Invited members can change temperatures and schedules. Only the owner can remove devices or delete the household.

Remove a device

To remove a device, open it in the app and choose Remove device. Removing a device in the app does not reset the device itself. A removed thermostat keeps its schedules and Wi-Fi settings until it is reset.
