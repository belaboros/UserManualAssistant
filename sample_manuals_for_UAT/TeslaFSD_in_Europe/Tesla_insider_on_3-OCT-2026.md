# Tesla FSD approved in 8 European countries ahead of EU vote

Tesla's Full Self-Driving (Supervised) is now approved in eight European countries, with the EU-wide vote still months away. 

Croatia became the latest to sign off on September 29, joining the Netherlands, Lithuania, Estonia, Denmark, Belgium, Slovenia and Czechia. 

The approval came just weeks after the country's regulator had suggested it would wait for a decision at the EU level. 

Tesla says the rollout in Croatia will begin soon, using a custom European build of FSD v14 tailored to local road rules. 

It will initially be limited to vehicles with Hardware 4, while Hardware 3 owners wait for FSD v14 Lite. 

These country-by-country approvals are moving faster than Brussels. 

The EU-wide vote, originally expected on October 6, has been pushed back to December at the earliest, and approval across the bloc needs support from at least 15 member states. Until then, Tesla is building momentum one country at a time. 

One more approval would put FSD in a third of all EU members before the vote even takes place.
